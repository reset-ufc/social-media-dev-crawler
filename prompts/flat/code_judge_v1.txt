### Step 1. Evaluation Criteria

For each **code analysis** in the model output, you must evaluate it independently according to the following two dimensions:

1. **Misuse Detection Validity (0-1):**
   * **1** → The `has_misuse` field in llm decision accurately reflects whether the code(s) in that post contain cryptographic misuse.  
   * **0.5** → The justification is unclear or partially consistent with the evidence.  
   * **0** → The misuse judgment is clearly incorrect or unsupported by the code content.

2. **Classification Accuracy Validity (0-1):**
   * **1** → The `category` and `subtype` fields in the `misuses` list correctly describe the misuse type(s) present.  
   * **0.5** → Partially accurate, vague, or overly broad classification.  
   * **0** → Incorrect or irrelevant classification category/subtype.

You should consider the model's reasoning, evidence quotes, and rationale consistency with known cryptographic standards and misuse taxonomies (e.g., static IVs, weak keys, insecure modes, poor randomness, etc.).
