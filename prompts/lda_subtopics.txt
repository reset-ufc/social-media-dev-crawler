# TASK: Interpret and Name LDA Topics

You are an expert in **topic modeling**, **LDA interpretation**, and **software engineering/software-security concepts**.  
You will receive a list of LDA topics, each represented by its most relevant words.

Your job is to:
1. **Infer the underlying semantic theme** of each topic.  
2. **Assign a short, clear, human-readable name** to every topic.  
3. **Explain briefly** why this name fits (1-2 sentences max).  

The LDA model training was performed on a corpus of Stack Overflow posts related to cryptography, limited to the scope of "{subtopic}". 
The analysis therefore requires the interpretation of the LDA results to identify and categorize the emerging subtopics.

For each word in LDA output, consider its weight to infer how significant it is within the set.

LDA OUTPUT
{model_output}
